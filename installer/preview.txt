Sentinel is an unsigned development preview.

It checks files locally against known threat hashes and helps you review Windows protection. It does not yet replace a tested full antivirus product. Keep Microsoft Defender enabled.

Setup installs for your account, adds a Start-menu shortcut and registers an uninstaller in Windows Settings. It does not enable automatic startup, monitoring, notifications, AI, or server connections.

Uninstall removes installed program files and this installation's daily scan. Your Sentinel settings, scan history, encrypted credentials, quarantine, and explicitly configured firewall blocks are preserved.
